# Uncertain Cards Report

Pairs where the decimal GT and integer GT disagree in *direction* —
i.e. the rater consensus flipped the sign during aggregation.
These pairs represent genuine annotation uncertainty and are used as
a diagnostic lens on model behaviour under ambiguity.

> **Accuracy** and **F1** are computed against the integer GT (the final
> consensus label), since that is what models were trained to predict.
> The decimal GT determines *which* pairs enter each mask.
